Why it exists

Real instances, not wrappers

A separate app bundle per account, re-signed with its own identity. The usual trick — one binary, a different --user-data-dir — separates the data but not the identity, and macOS keys sign-in and OAuth callbacks on identity.

Honest numbers

Claude already records its own plan utilisation. TrackMyUsage reads the same figures the app does, for every account at once, rather than estimating from token logs. A reading older than half an hour is marked, not shown as current.

Ambient by design

When everything is under 80% the wallpaper panel dims to a whisper. When something is hot it grows, brightens and leads with the offender. Silence is information too.

Adapters are absent rather than stubbed. A parser written from a remembered API shape is indistinguishable from a correct one until it reports the wrong number, so each one is written against a real recorded response.
